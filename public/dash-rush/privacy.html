Privacy Policy for Dash Rush

Last updated: November 5, 2026
Android package: dashrush.com

Jovexa Labs publishes Dash Rush, an Android running game. This policy explains the information handled by the game and its advertising service.

Game information stored on your device

The game does not require an account or sign-in. Scores, coins, unlocked cosmetics, missions, rewards, settings and other game progress are stored in the app's local storage. We do not operate a game account server or receive this saved progress. Device or operating-system backup settings may affect whether local app data is backed up or restored.

The game does not ask you to provide your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. It has no chat or user-generated content features.

Advertising and information processed by Google

Dash Rush uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads between rounds and optional rewarded ads for in-game benefits. Google may collect or process information including IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostic information and identifiers permitted by the SDK configuration. This processing supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service reliability. We do not sell your saved game progress.

All users receive the app's child-directed advertising configuration: non-personalised ad requests, child-directed and under-age-of-consent tags, and a maximum ad content rating of G. The app removes the Android advertising ID permission. Non-personalised ads still involve data processing; these settings do not mean that no information is collected.

Google handles advertising information under its own policies. Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children and families

Dash Rush is intended for players ages 6 and older, including children. The game does not request personal details from children or provide personalised advertising. The advertising protections above apply to all players. Parents and guardians can contact us with privacy concerns.

Network access and external links

The core game can be played offline. Network access is used to request ads and determine connectivity; some ad-supported rewards require a connection and an available ad. Opening a privacy or support link may launch a browser or another app, whose privacy practices also apply.

Retention and deletion

You can delete local progress by clearing Dash Rush's app storage in Android settings. Uninstalling removes the local installation's app data, subject to device backup and restore settings. Google's policies govern retention and deletion of information processed by Google. We cannot directly delete Google's advertising records.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request. Please do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal details.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its privacy practices change. Updates will be posted here with a revised date.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Jovexa Labs at support@jovexalabs.com.
