How to join

  1. Install Jovexa Move from Google Play on your Android phone and sign in.
  2. Open the invitation link on that phone. Jovexa Move shows the group and asks if you want to join.
  3. If the link opens in your browser instead, copy it. In the app, go to Groups, tap ⋮, choose Join with an invitation link and paste it.

Invitation links work for 30 days. In a private group, the owner or an admin approves each request to join.

Jovexa Move is for people aged 18 or over. Everyone in a group follows the Community Guidelines.
