Jovexa Move Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 November 2026 · Version: 2026-11-13
App: Jovexa Move for Android (package com.jovexalabs.move)

1. Who we are

Jovexa Move is an Android app for counting steps, tracking walks and runs, earning Move Points, joining walking and running groups and contributing to a community map. It is operated by Jovexa Labs ("we", "us"). For anything about your privacy, write to support@jovexalabs.com.

This policy covers the Jovexa Move app, its backend service and these pages. It does not cover the shops or websites of partner merchants, or Google services you use outside the app.

2. The short version

  • You sign in with your Google account. We keep your email address, your name and the username you choose.
  • The first time you sign in, the app asks you to accept the Terms of Service and this policy. Until you do, the app collects nothing and we hold nothing about you except the account that signing in created.
  • We collect step counts and summaries of your walks and runs to show your activity and award Move Points. Your GPS route stays on your phone.
  • Location permission is optional. The app never asks for background location.
  • Activity is checked automatically for signs of cheating, and our staff may review it.
  • In groups, other members see your username, your messages, the progress you share and your verified steps on the group leaderboard.
  • People without Move Premium see ads. Personalised ads and analytics are off unless you turn them on. Crash reports are always sent.
  • You can export your data and delete your account in the app, or ask us by email.
  • Our servers are in Singapore, and Google processes some data worldwide.
  • Jovexa Move is only for people aged 18 or over.

4. What stays on your phone

  • GPS routes. If you allow location, the app uses GPS while you track a walk or run, to measure distance. The route and the individual location points are stored only on your phone. We receive only the summary figures in section 3.
  • No background location. Location is used only while a tracked session is running (shown by a notification) or while the map shows your position.
  • Step counting works without location.
  • Health Connect. If you connect it, the app reads your step counts from it on your phone and never writes to it. We upload only the step totals described in section 3.
  • Offline activity waits on your phone until it can be uploaded. Activity older than 7 days is not accepted.
  • Your ad-consent choices are stored on your phone by Google's consent tool.

The map is drawn by the Google Maps SDK. To draw it, Google receives the area you look at, your IP address and device details, under Google's privacy policy. When the map shows your position, your location is used on your phone to draw it. The app also asks our server for the community places inside the area on screen.

Uninstalling the app removes what is stored on your phone. It does not delete your account.

5. Automated checks and human review

Move Points can be exchanged for rewards paid for by merchants, so we check that activity is genuine. Every upload is scored automatically for plausibility, for example speeds, step rates or stride lengths that are not humanly possible, timestamps in the future, overlapping sessions, distance with almost no steps and activity uploaded long after it happened. Each record is then marked verified, held for review or rejected. Held activity earns pending points and is reviewed by a member of our staff, who approves or rejects it. The app shows you the status of each record. Repeated or serious abuse can lead to suspension.

If you think a decision is wrong, write to support@jovexalabs.com and a person will look at it again.

6. Groups and community chat

  • What others see. Members of a group see your username, the messages you post, the progress cards you share, and your rank and verified steps of the last 7 days on the group leaderboard. Anyone using the app can find a group by its name and see its description and number of members.
  • Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They travel over encrypted connections and are stored on our servers so that members can read them. Our staff read a message when it has been reported or when needed to keep the community safe.
  • Reporting. When you report a message, we store the reason and your note. The author is not told who reported it. A message reported by three different people is hidden until a moderator decides.
  • Blocking. When you block someone, you no longer see their messages. They are not told.
  • Deleting. You can delete your own messages at any time; deleting your account deletes all of them.
  • Do not post personal information about yourself or others in groups. Read the Community Guidelines.

8. Where your data is processed

Our servers and database are in Singapore (Google Cloud region asia-southeast1). Google's services in section 7 may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers, we rely on Google's data processing terms, which include the standard contractual clauses approved for that purpose.

9. Advertising and consent

  • People without Move Premium see a banner on some screens and, after saving a tracked walk or run, sometimes a full-screen ad: at most 4 a day and at least 10 minutes apart. Move Premium is ad-free.
  • Ads never earn Move Points.
  • Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later in Settings.
  • Personalised ads are off by default. While they are off we ask Google for non-personalised ads only. Non-personalised ads still use some device information, such as your IP address and identifiers, to deliver ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud.
  • We do not pass your steps, sessions, Health Connect data, messages or Move Points history to the advertising service.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

Google explains how it uses data from apps that show its ads at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

10. Analytics and crash reports

Analytics is off by default; if you turn it on in Settings, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record how the app is used, and you can turn it off at any time. Crash reports are always sent through Firebase Crashlytics so that we can fix problems. They contain technical details only, never your messages or activity.

11. How long we keep your data

While your account is open we keep the data in section 3 for as long as the account exists, unless section 3 says otherwise. The app shows 30 days of activity history, or 365 days with Move Premium; older records stay on our servers while the account exists. We do not delete inactive accounts automatically. Database backups are kept for 7 days.

When you delete your account (in the app, or by asking us; see Delete your account), deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. We remove:

  • your email address, username and display name, and your Firebase sign-in identity;
  • your app installations and push tokens;
  • your hourly steps, sessions and daily summaries;
  • your challenge completions, notifications and settings;
  • your group messages, reports, memberships and blocks;
  • staff roles and merchant-staff links, if you had any;
  • your subscription records;
  • map contributions that were not approved.

At the same time unused vouchers are cancelled, all your Move Points are forfeited and open review cases are closed. We keep, without your personal details and linked only to an internal id: your points history and redemption records (accounting), fraud cases and the staff audit trail (preventing abuse), the record of which versions of the terms and this policy you accepted, a record that the deletion happened, and approved map contributions without the link to you. Deleted data disappears from backups when they expire, within 7 days.

Deleting your account does not affect your Google account, and it does not cancel a Move Premium subscription: cancel that in Google Play.

12. Your rights and choices

In the app you can:

  • see and correct your display name and username;
  • export your data in Settings, as a file with your profile, accepted documents, settings, devices, activity, points history, redemptions, map contributions, subscriptions, notifications, groups, messages, reports and blocks;
  • delete your account in Settings → Delete my account;
  • change your choices for analytics, personalised ads, showing your username and notifications, and your ad consent where Google's consent message applies;
  • withdraw the physical-activity, location and notification permissions in Android settings, and Health Connect access in the Health Connect app.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you the right to be informed, to access and receive a copy of your data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict or object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority, such as the National Privacy Commission in the Philippines (privacy.gov.ph) or the authority of your country. To use these rights, write to support@jovexalabs.com from the email address of your account. We answer within 30 days.

If your account is suspended, or you have not accepted the current documents, you can still view your profile, export your data and delete your account.

13. Legal bases

Where the law asks us to name one, we rely on: performing our contract with you (your account, activity, points, rewards, groups, Premium); your consent (analytics, personalised ads, marketing notifications, Health Connect and the phone permissions, which you can withdraw at any time); our legitimate interests in keeping the service safe and fair (fraud checks, security records, moderation); and legal obligations (accounting and lawful requests).

14. Children

Jovexa Move is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

15. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and our servers, permission checks on our servers for every request, a database role with only the access the service needs, encrypted voucher codes, encryption of the database at rest, daily backups, a permanent audit trail of staff actions, and automated and manual checks against abuse. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and change the effective date. When the version changes, the app shows you the new document and asks you to accept it.
